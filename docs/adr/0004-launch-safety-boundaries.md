# Restrict launch activity to adults and lower-risk categories

Guests may browse, but people must provide a basic profile and attest to being at least 18 before posting or applying. The launch excludes medical and childcare services, passenger transport, and hazardous work unless TaskHive later establishes suitable credentials and operating policies; this bounds safety exposure while the platform’s verification and support processes mature. Skills and portfolio are not required for the initial marketplace actions.
