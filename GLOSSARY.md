# TaskHive Glossary

Shared product language for the nationwide Philippines marketplace described in the SRS and refined through product-direction decisions.

## Marketplace

**Guest**:
A visitor who can browse public tasks and platform information but must sign in before taking marketplace actions.

**Marketplace participant**:
An account holder who attests to being at least 18 and can post tasks, bid or request work, or accept an assignment.

**Basic profile**:
The minimum participant profile required before posting or applying: name, verified email, and 18+ age attestation. A phone number is optional contact information and is not verified through TaskHive.

**Poster**:
A user who creates a task. The same account may also act as a Tasker.

**Tasker**:
A user who bids on or requests a task and may complete it after being selected by the Poster.

**Identity-verified Tasker**:
A Tasker who has passed the stronger identity check required before being assigned to a task.

**Verification-rejected Tasker**:
A Tasker who failed the provider check; they may browse but cannot apply or be assigned, and may submit one appeal for Admin review. Existing bids/requests are withdrawn and their Posters are notified; after a successful appeal the Tasker may submit again.

**Pending-verification Tasker**:
A Tasker who may browse, bid, or request fixed-price work but cannot be assigned until stronger identity verification is complete. Their applications remain visible to the Poster with a pending status and cannot be approved yet.

**Task conversation**:
A conversation tied to one open task between its Poster and a signed-in user; it may begin before a bid or fixed-price request.

**Tasker application**:
A bid on a bidding task or a request on a fixed-price task; a Tasker may withdraw it until assignment.

**Material task change**:
A change to title/scope, budget, deadline, service location, fulfillment mode, or hiring mode that invalidates existing bids/requests and notifies their authors.

**Draft task deletion**:
A Poster may permanently remove a task that has not been published.

**Published task cancellation**:
A Poster may cancel an open published task, which removes it from browsing while preserving its task and application history.

**Bidding task**:
A task where Taskers submit proposals and the Poster manually selects one Tasker.

**Fixed-price task**:
A task with a price set by the Poster; Taskers request it and the Poster manually selects one Tasker.

**Assignment**:
The accepted relationship between one task, its Poster, and exactly one selected Tasker.

**Hiring mode**:
The way a Poster selects a Tasker: bidding or fixed-price request.

**Fulfillment mode**:
Whether a task requires in-person work at a service location or can be completed remotely online.

**Nationwide Philippines marketplace**:
TaskHive is available across the Philippines and supports both local and remote tasks.

**Remote task**:
A task in a platform-approved category that can be completed online without an in-person service location. First-release remote categories are tutoring, writing, design, and translation.

**Local task**:
A task that requires in-person work at a specified service location. Taskers see an approximate area before hire and the exact address after payment succeeds and the assignment becomes active.

**First-release local categories**:
Errands, delivery, cleaning, repairs, moving, and tutoring; high-risk or regulated categories are excluded unless a credential policy is later added.

**Task search area**:
The map origin and distance radius a Tasker chooses when searching for local tasks.

## Payments and trust

**Provider-managed payout**:
The Poster pays through a payment provider when hiring; after completion is confirmed, the provider routes the Tasker’s payout. This is not legal escrow.

**Payment retry hold**:
A 24-hour reservation for the selected Tasker and task while the Poster retries a failed payment. Other applications remain pending; if payment fails or expires, the task reopens with those applications retained.

**Platform fee**:
The fee TaskHive retains from the task payment before the remaining payout is routed to the Tasker. The initial rate is provisionally 5% of the task price, deducted from the Tasker’s payout.

**Post-hire cancellation request**:
A request from either the Poster or Tasker to stop an assigned task; work and payout are paused while support reviews the case.

**Cancellation resolution**:
The Admin’s support decision to issue a full refund, partial payout, or full payout based on the evidence of work completed.

**Launch Admin scope**:
Reports, disputes, verification exceptions, and basic user/task oversight. Advanced analytics and category management are deferred.

**Restricted task category**:
A category unavailable at launch because the work is regulated or high-risk, including medical/childcare services, passenger transport, and hazardous work unless a credential policy is added.

**Remote completion evidence**:
The deliverable submitted by a Tasker for remote work. Poster confirmation is required; either party may open a dispute before auto-release.

**Local completion evidence**:
A completion note from the Tasker; a photo or receipt may be attached optionally. Poster confirmation is required; either party may open a dispute before auto-release.

**Completion reminder**:
A notice sent to the Poster 24 hours after the Tasker marks a task complete if the Poster has not confirmed or disputed it.

**Completion auto-release**:
If neither party opens a dispute, payout releases 48 hours after the Tasker marks work complete; this also unlocks the two-way review flow.

**Two-way review window**:
Reviews remain hidden until both participants submit or 14 days pass; submitted reviews then become public.

**Marketplace notification**:
An in-app and email update for key events such as bids, chat messages, payment changes, task completion, and disputes.
